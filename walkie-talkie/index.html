Is Extero a walkie-talkie?

Yes. Push-to-talk between staff, from the same device, on your site Wi-Fi. Messages play from its speaker.

Do I need a phone?

No. Extero is battery-powered and works on your site Wi-Fi, with no phone and no pairing.

Can staff share one Extero?

Yes. Each person checks in on the device, so you still know who said what. Most teams need one Extero for each person working that day, not one for every employee.

Do I need Individual Intelligence?

Only for people who want Extero to write their records. Without it, Extero records hands-free into the software you already use and gives you WiFi Walkie-Talkies and 10 AI voice commands, reminders and timers a month. Individual Intelligence is per user: it adds AI records and unlimited AI voice commands, reminders and timers (fair use applies), and includes free AI records for support staff.
