Why dental office communication breaks down

Your hands are gloved and busy, your team is spread across operatories, sterilization and the front desk, and the usual tools were not built for that.

Walking down the hall to ask

The doctor is needed in hygiene, the next patient is seated, a tray is missing. Someone leaves the chair or the desk to go and say it in person.

A light or a page says little

A light panel or a dental office paging system tells you that someone needs you. It does not tell you why, or what to bring.

Notes wait until your hands are free

You cannot type with gloves on. Clinical notes get written between patients or at the end of the day, from memory.

A dental office communication system with three jobs

Extero clips on, has a microphone and a speaker built in, and works on your office Wi-Fi with no phone.

Capture: say the note at the chair

Where Extero fits in a dental office

A good fit

  • Offices with several operatories, where the front desk, assistants, hygienists and doctors need to reach each other.
  • Teams that want to say a note chairside and send the transcript to software they already use.

Not the right fit

Your device at a glance.

The main button on the front and the agent button on the side do most of the work. The ring of lights tells you what the device is doing.

Speaker

Spoken replies, messages, alert tones and the low-battery warning. A reply from your AI assistant plays by itself, and the ring shows cyan while it plays.

LED ring

Green ring: recording. Cyan: a reply or message is playing. Blue: Bluetooth pairing mode. Red ring: reconnecting to Wi-Fi. Dark: idle and connected.

Main button

Hold about 2 s: turn on. 1 tap: start recording, tap again to stop. 3 taps: send a help request to your team. 4 taps: play a waiting message. Hold 1.5 s: Bluetooth pairing on or off.

Charging port

Charges the battery; plugging in also turns the device on. The ring glows red slowly while charging and goes dark when the battery is full.

Volume + / −

Each press changes the speaker volume one step; the lights show the level in white. Hold both together for 5 seconds to turn the device off.

Agent button

Press once, speak, press again. A spoken answer plays from the device. Double-press to check the battery.

What systems do dental offices use?

For reaching each other inside the office, the common choices are a light panel, a dental office paging system, a dental office intercom, two-way radios or team chat software. Extero is another kind of dental office communication system: a clip-on Wi-Fi walkie-talkie that also records notes hands-free.

Dental office light system or voice: which should we choose?

A light system signals silently that someone is needed, without the reason. Extero carries the words: a waiting message shows as a cyan double pulse on the ring of lights, and four taps play it from the speaker. Choose by whether your team needs the reason or only the signal.

Is there a difference between a walkie-talkie and a two-way radio?

In everyday use the two names describe the same kind of handheld radio. Extero is neither: it is a Wi-Fi walkie-talkie, so push-to-talk messages travel over your office Wi-Fi and it works only where that Wi-Fi reaches.

How much does a dental AI scribe cost?

It depends on the product. Extero is $299 per device, or $199 with an Annual plan, then $19 per device per month; Individual Intelligence, which adds AI records, is $30 per user per month. Prices are in USD, before shipping and taxes.

Do I need any additional hardware?

Extero is the hardware: a clip-on device with a microphone and a speaker built in. To set it up, turn it on and connect it to your office Wi-Fi from the Extero app over Bluetooth. After that it works on your Wi-Fi with no phone and no pairing.

Can it integrate with my practice management software?

You can send recordings and transcripts anywhere with the API, webhooks or the MCP server. Whether they land inside your practice management software depends on what that software accepts, so bring that question to a demo. Check software compatibility.

What is an AI dental scribe?

The term usually means a tool that listens during a visit and drafts the clinical note. If you are looking for a dental AI scribe, Extero covers the hands-free part as a wearable: one press records, the recording is transcribed, and Individual Intelligence adds AI records.

Is Extero right for our dental office?

It fits an office with Wi-Fi in every room whose team wants voice messages and hands-free notes from one wearable. It does not fit rooms without Wi-Fi coverage, and it does not chart by voice inside your practice software.
