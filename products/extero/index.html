What is Extero?

Extero is the AI wearable for hands-on teams. It clips on, with a microphone and a speaker built in. Take notes hands-free, talk to your team and ask AI. No phone needed.

How much does Extero cost?

The Extero device is $299, or $199 with an Annual plan. Then every device is $19 per month. Individual Intelligence adds $30 per user per month, and Team Intelligence adds $100 per team per month, with the first month free. Billed monthly or yearly. Prices are in USD, before shipping and taxes.

Do I need Individual Intelligence?

Only for people who want Extero to write their records. Without it, Extero records hands-free into the software you already use and gives you WiFi Walkie-Talkies and 10 AI voice commands, reminders and timers a month. Individual Intelligence is per user: it adds AI records and unlimited AI voice commands, reminders and timers (fair use applies), and includes free AI records for support staff.

What is Team Intelligence?

Team Intelligence is one subscription for the team: advanced AI reports, the task tracker and community plugins, unlimited storage, the Flowchart/Whiteboard and priority support. It is $100 per team per month, the first month is free and you can cancel anytime. Black Friday: order Extero by Nov 30 and your first year of Team Intelligence is free.

Example: what does a team pay?

Three devices, with Individual Intelligence for two users: 3 × $19 + 2 × $30 = $117 per month, plus $199 per device once with an Annual plan. Team Intelligence adds $100 per month for the team.

Does Extero work with my software?

Yes. Send recordings and transcripts anywhere with the API, webhooks or the MCP server. Tell us which software you use and we will confirm the setup. Check software compatibility.

Do I need a phone?

No. You set Extero up once from the Extero app over Bluetooth. After that it works on your site Wi-Fi with no phone: recording, team messages and AI.

How do I set up Extero?

Turn it on (plug in the charger, or hold the main button for about 2 seconds), connect it to your site Wi-Fi from the Extero app over Bluetooth, and it is ready when the ring goes dark. The Extero quickstart guide walks you through the six steps, the buttons and what every light means.

Can I cancel an annual subscription?

Cancelling an annual subscription stops the next renewal. Unused prepaid months are not refunded.

When will my Extero ship?

Extero is on pre-order: your order joins our next shipping batch, and we email you tracking as soon as it ships. You pay for the device and shipping when you order, and your plan is charged the day your first Extero comes online, at the latest 180 days after payment.

Can staff share one Extero?

Yes. Each person checks in on the device, so you still know who said what. Most teams need one Extero for each person working that day, not one for every employee.

Do you offer volume pricing?

Yes. Book a call for custom pricing.

Where is Extero made?

Extero is designed, assembled and tested in Canada by the Extero team.

How do I order?

Pick your number of devices, how many users want Individual Intelligence, whether to add Team Intelligence, and your billing period: the price updates as you go. Then continue to checkout. You enter your shipping address, pick a delivery option and pay by card on our secure Stripe checkout. Your Extero account is created as soon as the payment goes through.
