Your device at a glance.

The main button on the front and the agent button on the side do most of the work. The ring of lights tells you what the device is doing.

Speaker

Spoken replies, messages, alert tones and the low-battery warning. A reply from your AI assistant plays by itself, and the ring shows cyan while it plays.

LED ring

Green ring: recording. Cyan: a reply or message is playing. Blue: Bluetooth pairing mode. Red ring: reconnecting to Wi-Fi. Dark: idle and connected.

Main button

Hold about 2 s: turn on. 1 tap: start recording, tap again to stop. 3 taps: send a help request to your team. 4 taps: play a waiting message. Hold 1.5 s: Bluetooth pairing on or off.

Charging port

Charges the battery; plugging in also turns the device on. The ring glows red slowly while charging and goes dark when the battery is full.

Volume + / −

Each press changes the speaker volume one step; the lights show the level in white. Hold both together for 5 seconds to turn the device off.

Agent button

Press once, speak, press again. A spoken answer plays by itself within about 90 seconds. Double-press to check the battery.

What is Extero?

Extero is the AI wearable for hands-on teams. It clips on, with a microphone and a speaker built in. Take notes hands-free, talk to your team and ask AI. No phone needed.

Is Extero a walkie-talkie?

Yes. Push-to-talk between staff, from the same device, on your site Wi-Fi. Messages play from its speaker.

Do I need a phone?

No. Extero is battery-powered and works on your site Wi-Fi, with no phone and no pairing.

Does Extero work with my software?

Yes. Use Extero with your existing software: you can send recordings anywhere with the API, webhooks or the MCP server.

Who decides when it records?

Your staff. One press routes speech to the record, the team or AI. Staff control when it records.

Can staff share one Extero?

Yes. Each person checks in on the device, so you still know who said what.

When will my Extero ship?

Extero is on pre-order: your order joins our next shipping batch, and we email you tracking as soon as it ships.

How do I order?

On the Extero page, choose your billing period and number of devices, then continue to checkout. You enter your shipping address, pick a delivery option and pay by card on our secure Stripe checkout.

Where is Extero made?

Extero is designed, assembled and tested in Canada.
